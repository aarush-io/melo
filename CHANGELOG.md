# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Add SoundCloud: search, tracks, playlists, albums, artists and stations without an account, with downloads and offline cache. Link a public profile to see its likes and playlists.
- Add a Russian translation.
- Keep bottom navigation labels on one line on narrow screens and at large font sizes.
- Add My Wave, a tab that turns the track you are playing into an endless queue of similar music. It follows what you play through, like or skip, keeps one version of each song and spaces out artists. With nothing playing it starts from your history, your liked songs or the time of day. The wave keeps growing while the app is closed and music plays on, and keeps its own order when shuffle is on.
- Bring the open app back from the playback notification instead of opening a second copy of it.
- Move Search from the bottom bar to the first button of the Home header. A start tab saved as Search now opens Home.

## Melo 1.2.0

### Home

- Speed Dials refresh more smoothly, with improved Lucky and History tiles.
- The Mixes dial can show Spotify playlists when your account is connected.

### Playback

- Play a song on its own and radio recommendations will follow.

### Player

- Track titles and artist names stay visible more reliably.

### Settings

- Account options and settings are easier to find.

## Melo 1.0.0 — Public launch

- Release Melo as a standalone Android app with the in.melo application ID.
- Publish the complete application source and signed ARM64 APK.
- Connect in-app update checks to Melo's public GitHub Releases feed.
- Bring the dot-matrix identity, monochrome interface, colourful artwork, and refined start page together in one release.
